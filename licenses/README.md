# Included licence texts

This directory accompanies CalcioTotale source copies and executable packages. The files apply only to the corresponding third-party material; they do not replace the proprietary terms in the project-root `LICENSE`. The separate `data/` football content package is outside the scope of that proprietary licence and is not covered by the component licence texts collected here.

The Italian and English CalcioTotale localisations are project material covered by the root licence. The language-selector PNG files are not represented by `MIT-FLAG-ICONS.txt`; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for the distinction between those project-provided assets and applicable upstream `flag-icons` SVG files.

- `PYTHON-3.12-LICENSE.txt` — Python 3.12 and software incorporated into that distribution.
- `LGPL-3.0.txt` and `GPL-3.0.txt` — general GNU terms relevant to the PySide6/Qt Community distribution used by automated builds.
- `PYINSTALLER-COPYING.txt` — PyInstaller, including its bootloader exception and runtime-hook terms.
- `MIT-CMU-PILLOW.txt` — Pillow, used during packaging.
- `APACHE-2.0.txt` — applicable Google interface icons.
- `OFL-1.1.txt` — Exo 2 and Oxanium.
- `MIT-FLAG-ICONS.txt` — applicable `flag-icons` SVG material.

`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` is the maintained inventory and explains where each component is used. Every release must also be checked against the exact dependency versions listed in its `BUILD_COMPONENTS.txt`; Qt/PySide packages may require additional module-specific notices or compliance material beyond these general licence texts.
